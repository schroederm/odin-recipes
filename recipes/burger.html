Cheeseburger

If you're in the mood for convenience, a good burger is hard to top: it's fast to make and you don't neet any cutlery.

It's also easy to make a vegetarian version of this dish these days – vegetarian burger patties are available in stores plentiful and in good quality that easily matches meat patties.

Ingredients

  • 1 Burger Patty
  • 1 Sesame bun
  • Some salad
  • Burger sauce
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 slice of Cheddar

Steps

  1. Fry or grill the burger patty for 5 minutes on medium heat.
  2. Meanwhile, slice the bun in half and toast it slightly.
  3. Spread the burger sauce on the lower part of the bun.
  4. Wash the salad and prepare leaves to your liking.
  5. Cut the tomato into slices.
  6. Put the finished patty on the lower bun. Add cheese directly on top. Add tomato and salad slices to your liking.
