Vegetarian Lasagna

As the days grow colder, some hearty comfort food can warm your soul after coming home in the dark from a long day of work. I find that lasagna is one of the best dishes for this, as the tomatoes in its sauce pack a hefty dose of umami while the cheese and bechamel bring along both fat and salt to hit even more sweet spots in your taste profile.

The minced meat and noodle plates, at the same time, add just the right texture and chewiness, so that each bite presents its mixture of flavours anew. Add just the right spices and you will feel like sitting in Naples even when you're spending a winter night in Helsinki.

Ingredients

  • 15 Lasagna Plates
  • 300g shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 500 ml tomato purée
  • 600g minced meat substitute
  • 2 carrots
  • grated parmesan ad libertum
  • 2 teaspoons oregano
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • salt and pepper
  • some oil

Steps

  1. Dice onion and garlic and fry with oil on medium heat until golden.
  2. Dice the carrot.
  3. Add meat substitute and carrot and fry for five more minutes until they start to brown
  4. Add tomato purée and cook for three more minutes. Add oregano, and salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Preheat the oven to 190ºC.
  6. In a casserole dish, alternate stacking a layer of lasagna plates and a layer of sauce. End on a lasagna plate.
  7. Spread the shredded mozzarella on top.
  8. Bake for 25 minutes.
