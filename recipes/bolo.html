Spaghetti Bolognese

Another easy to make comfort food. The sauce can get really intense and full of umami if you let it simmer of an afternoon or so after it's done. Just keep an eye on it and add some water again if necessary.

This is another recipe where it's practically no effort to make the vegetarian version and it will be identical in taste and texture if you find good vegetarian minced meat, which a lot of stores have now. Ask the manager if yours doesn't.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 Carrot
  • 500g minced meat
  • Salt and pepper
  • 200ml vegetable broth
  • 70g tomato purée
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 500g spaghetti noodles

Steps

  1. Peel onion, garlic and carrot and chop into small cubes.
  2. Cook the minced meat in a pan on medium heat until it starts to brown. Add salt and pepper.
  3. Add onions, garlic, carrot to the mix. Cook two more minutes.
  4. Deglaze with broth. Add tomato purée, organo and crushed tomatoes. Boil down for 40 minutes.
  5. Cook the spaghetti al dente. Strain and serve with the sauce.
